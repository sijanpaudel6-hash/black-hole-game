SPAGHETTIFICATION

TIDAL FORCES EXCEED SELF-GRAVITATION

When an object falls toward a black hole, the immense difference in gravitational pull between its near and far sides stretches it vertically and compresses it horizontally. Known formally as tidal disruption, this extreme tidal gradient elongates entire stars into noodle-thin streams of incandescent plasma before they cross the event horizon.
